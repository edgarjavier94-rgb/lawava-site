The filter

Six questions, asked before anything reaches your team.

These are marketing filters on a form, not legal opinions. We decide who is worth the call. Your attorneys decide who has a claim.

01 / Injury

Was the person hurt, or was it only the car? A property damage only lead is not a case, and it costs your team the same hour to work.

02 / Treatment

Did they get medical treatment, and how soon after the crash? A treatment gap changes everything downstream.

03 / Fault

Was the other driver clearly at fault? A clean rear end reads very differently from a disputed intersection.

04 / Coverage

Is there insurance to collect from, or is there uninsured and underinsured coverage on their side?

05 / Timeframe

Is the crash inside the window your state allows? One date field on the form removes the ones that are not.

06 / Representation

Have they already signed with another attorney? Right now you are paying to talk to people who hired someone else last week.

In a no fault state the form does more of the work, which makes getting it right worth more, not less. We build the filter set around the state you practice in and confirm the thresholds with your attorneys before a single dollar runs.
